Offline sample foods and recipes still use illustrative generic estimates. Live packaged-food values come from Open Food Facts and must be verified against the package. Live scanning depends on an HTTPS context and Android/Chrome camera access. The saved-photo picker does not require camera permission. The photo reader uses its own offline UPC-A / EAN-13 / EAN-8 decoder, and works without the experimental BarcodeDetector API. For best results keep the bars vertical, centered, sharp and well-lit. If a photo cannot be decoded, enter the printed digits. There is no image recognition, retailer inventory, price comparison, or clinical validation.
